About the Akut Bom Party

The Akut Bom Party (ABP) is founded on the principles of inclusiveness, African cooperation, equality, decentralization, grassroots governance, and national unity.

ABP seeks a South Sudan in which every citizen belongs, every community matters, government is close to the people, and national development serves the whole country.

Our Fundamental Philosophy

The philosophy of the Akut Bom Party is built around seven fundamental principles that guide our vision for governance, society and national development.

1. Kondialism — Inclusiveness

Kondialism is a Nuer-language concept meaning inclusiveness. ABP believes that every citizen and every community has a legitimate place in the political, economic, social and cultural life of South Sudan.

No citizen or community should be excluded from the national project because of ethnicity, language, region, gender, social status or political affiliation.

2. Ujamaaaism — African Cooperative Socialism

Ujamaaaism represents ABP's concept of an African cooperative socialism adapted to South Sudanese realities.

It emphasizes cooperation, community development, productive enterprise, cooperatives, agriculture, local industries, small businesses and the responsible use of national resources.

3. Egalitarianism — Equality of Humanity

ABP recognizes the fundamental equality and dignity of all human beings. Every citizen should enjoy equal citizenship, equal protection under the law and fair access to public opportunities and services.

4. Complete Decentralization

ABP proposes a fundamental decentralization of government authority. The present system of states, counties and payams would be replaced by a simpler structure that brings government closer to the people.

5. Two-Tier Government

ABP proposes two principal levels of government:

National Government: based in Juba and responsible for matters of national importance.

Boma Government: the principal grassroots level of administration, participation and local development.

6. Juba and the Boma

ABP's proposed governance structure places Juba and the Boma at the center of national and grassroots administration.

Juba represents the national level, while the Boma represents the people at the grassroots. The objective is to reduce unnecessary bureaucracy and bring decision-making closer to citizens.

7. Recognition and Compensation of Mothers for Child-Rearing

ABP recognizes raising and caring for children as essential work that sustains families, communities and the future of the nation.

The Party proposes that eligible mothers and primary caregivers who undertake child-rearing responsibilities should be considered for a publicly supported child-rearing allowance administered through the Boma level of government.

ABP regards investment in mothers and children as investment in the country's future human capacity.
